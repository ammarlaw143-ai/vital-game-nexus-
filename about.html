About Us

Nexus Gaming is a premium destination for news, guides, reviews and a database that spans every major platform — built by a small team of passionate writers and engineers.

Our Mission

We exist to help every gamer find their next obsession — whether that's the next AAA blockbuster, a hidden indie gem, the perfect Minecraft mod, or the most efficient GTA Online business. We connect players to official sources with editorial integrity at the center of everything we publish.

Our Story

Nexus Gaming started as a Discord server full of players arguing about which Elden Ring build was actually broken. It grew into a small blog, then a database, and now a full publication covering everything from AAA release calendars to niche Minecraft command tutorials. The team is still small, still gamer-owned, and still writes every word by hand.

Editorial Standards

Every review reflects the writer's honest opinion. Sponsored content is labeled at the top of the article. We only link to official stores and creators — never to pirated content. When we get something wrong, we correct it publicly and note the change.
