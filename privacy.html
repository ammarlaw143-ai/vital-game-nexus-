Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Nexus Gaming collects when you visit the site, how it is used, and the choices you have. This page is maintained by the Nexus Gaming team and may be updated as the site evolves.

1. Information We Collect

We try to collect as little as possible. In practice this means:

  • Anonymous analytics — page views, referrer, approximate country, device type, and browser. This is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.
  • Form submissions — anything you type into the contact form (name, email, subject, message). This is only used to reply to you.
  • Newsletter signups — the email address you provide, plus the fact that you subscribed. Nothing else.
  • Local storage — your favorite games list is stored in your own browser. We never see it.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To understand which articles and guides are useful so we can write more of them.
  • To reply to messages, tips, or partnership requests you send us.
  • To deliver the newsletter, if you asked for it.
  • To keep the site secure and detect abuse.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We use only what's necessary:

  • Essential — required for the site to function (e.g. remembering that you closed a banner).
  • Analytics — a privacy-respecting analytics script that measures traffic without identifying you.
  • Local storage — used to save your favorites and reading preferences on your device.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time from your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Game pages link to official stores and publisher sites (Steam, Epic Games, PlayStation Store, Xbox, Nintendo eShop, and others). When you click those links you leave Nexus Gaming and become subject to that site's own privacy policy. We are not responsible for what those sites do with your data.

Images may be served from official press kits, Wikipedia, or a game's storefront CDN. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

5. Data Retention

Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to answer you and are then deleted. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is aggregated and retained in summary form.

6. Your Rights

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your data.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter (there's also a link in every email).

7. Children's Privacy

8. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect the site, but no online service is ever 100% secure. Please don't send us sensitive information (passwords, payment details, ID documents) — we don't need it.
